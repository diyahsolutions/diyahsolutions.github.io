Health & wellness

Patient journey automation for a functional medicine practice

A complete GoHighLevel system that qualifies health-coaching applicants, books consultations, sends reminders, nurtures the undecided and recovers pending payments, all on autopilot.

Industry
Holistic health & functional medicine
Location
Illinois, USA
Platform
GoHighLevel
Role
Full CRM implementation & lead-journey architect

The challenge

The practice sells high-ticket health coaching. Applications came in, but qualification, booking and follow-up were manual. Prospects dropped off between applying and booking, reminders were inconsistent, and there was no system for people who weren’t ready yet or who stalled at payment.

What we built

  • Metabolic application & qualification. A survey-triggered workflow that captures key health indicators, then branches leads into High priority, Nurture, Not financially ready and At risk paths with tags, opportunities and instant SMS and email.
  • Booking confirmation & reminders. When a consult is booked, the team gets a task, the lead gets an instant SMS confirmation, and a timed email and SMS reminder cadence protects the show-up rate.
  • Qualified-but-not-booked nurture. Tag-based sequences that pair strategic waits with value-driven emails and conversion-focused SMS to bring warm leads back to the calendar.
  • Global reply notifications. The moment any contact replies, staff are alerted internally, so hot conversations never sit unanswered.
  • Not-financially-ready & payment recovery. A long-game nurture for prospects who aren’t ready yet, plus a financing and payment-pending loop that sends timely reminders until checkout is complete.
  • High-converting VSL landing page. A direct-response page with a pain-point hook, embedded video sales letter, video and text testimonial grids and a clear 4-step method breakdown.

What changed

  • Manual calendar booking and chasing replaced by automated qualification and scheduling
  • Every applicant routed to the right journey based on urgency and budget
  • No reply or pending payment left without a follow-up
  • One connected system the team can see and manage in GoHighLevel

AI & integrations

AI lead qualification with Make, OpenAI & GoHighLevel

An instant-mode Make.com scenario that qualifies inbound leads with ChatGPT, prevents duplicates and creates or updates GoHighLevel contacts and opportunities via the API.

Type
AI automation & integration
Platforms
Make.com, OpenAI, GoHighLevel, Google Sheets
Speed
10 operations in under 4 seconds
Status
Live, instant mode

The challenge

Leads were arriving from outside GoHighLevel with inconsistent information. The team needed every lead qualified the same way, logged safely, and placed in the right pipeline, without creating duplicate contacts.

What we built

  • Custom webhook intake. Receives inbound lead data the moment it is submitted.
  • AI qualification with OpenAI. ChatGPT generates a structured qualification output, which a text parser turns into clean fields.
  • Backup logging. Every record is also written to Google Sheets as a safety net.
  • Duplicate prevention. A GoHighLevel Search Contacts step checks for an existing record, and a router sends new and existing contacts down separate paths.
  • API create & update. HTTP POST calls create new contacts and pipeline opportunities. HTTP PUT updates existing records. Duplicates are skipped cleanly.

What changed

  • 10 operations per run, completed in under 4 seconds
  • Consistent AI-driven qualification for every inbound lead
  • No duplicate contacts in the CRM
  • Qualified leads land in the right pipeline stage instantly

Real estate

End-to-end lead workflow for a real estate title team

A multi-branch GoHighLevel workflow that takes real estate leads from first touch to booked appointment, with a dedicated follow-up loop for anyone who hasn’t booked yet.

Industry
Real estate & title services
Platform
GoHighLevel
Scope
Lead workflow + booking follow-up
Focus
Speed-to-lead & appointments

The challenge

Leads from agents, buyers and sellers needed different follow-up, and anyone who didn’t book straight away tended to go quiet. The team wanted one reliable flow instead of manual reminders.

What we built

  • End-to-end lead workflow. A wide, multi-branch workflow that routes each lead type into its own sequence of emails, texts, waits and internal tasks.
  • Follow-up to book. A dedicated workflow that keeps following up with leads who haven’t booked, and stops automatically once they do.
  • Clear naming system. Coded workflow names (R003, A0001…) so the team can find, audit and extend automations easily.

What changed

  • Every lead type gets the right message at the right time
  • Unbooked leads are followed up consistently until they schedule
  • An organised account that is easy to maintain and scale

First-time homebuyer webinar automation

Registration, confirmation, reminders and attended/no-show follow-up for a multi-session homebuyer education webinar series.

Industry
Real estate & homebuyer education
Platform
GoHighLevel
Scope
4-session webinar series
Focus
Show-up rate & follow-up

The challenge

A four-part webinar series meant four sets of registrations, confirmations, reminders and follow-ups. Doing it manually was error-prone, and attendees and no-shows needed very different messages.

What we built

  • Webinar registration workflow. Captures registrations and enrols people into the right session sequence.
  • Session confirmations & reminders. Timed confirmation and reminder messages for each session to maximise attendance.
  • Attended / no-show branching. Separate paths for attendees and no-shows after every session: replay links, next steps or a gentle nudge.
  • Organised session folders. Workflows grouped per session, plus DND handling and a broker’s open automation.

What changed

  • Every session runs the same reliable way without manual sends
  • Attendees and no-shows each get relevant follow-up
  • A reusable structure for future webinar series

Smart follow-up automation that books appointments

Type
Follow-up automation
Platform
GoHighLevel
Focus
Engagement-based branching
Goal
Booked appointments

The challenge

Leads engage in different ways: some open emails, some reply to texts, some go quiet. A single linear sequence was either too pushy or too passive.

What we built
