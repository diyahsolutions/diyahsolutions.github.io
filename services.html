CRM automation

What is CRM automation?

It’s the set of rules and workflows inside your CRM that do the repetitive work for you: capturing and routing new leads, sending follow-up emails and texts, moving deals through your pipeline, booking calls, reminding people and reporting on what’s working. Done well, no lead is ever left without a next step.

Do I have to switch to GoHighLevel to work with you?

No. We start with the CRM you already use (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho, Follow Up Boss or another platform) and automate it properly. GoHighLevel is our specialist platform, so if it would genuinely serve you better we’ll say so and explain why. The decision is always yours.

Which CRMs and tools do you work with?

GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho CRM and real estate CRMs such as Follow Up Boss and Lofty; email platforms including Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Omnisend and MailerLite; and Zapier, Make, webhooks and APIs to connect everything else.

What is lead management automation?

It’s everything that happens between someone enquiring and someone booking: capturing the lead from every source, de-duplicating it, assigning an owner, sending an instant first response, scoring or qualifying it, and alerting your team when it’s hot.

GoHighLevel

What is GoHighLevel and who is it for?

GoHighLevel (also called HighLevel or GHL) is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform. It combines a CRM, pipelines, workflow automation, SMS, email, calendars, funnels, websites, forms, reputation management, memberships and AI tools in one place.

It’s popular with marketing agencies, coaches and consultants, real estate teams, clinics and med spas, home and field service businesses, SaaS companies and local businesses that want to replace several tools with one system.

What does a GoHighLevel expert do?

A GoHighLevel expert plans, builds and maintains the systems inside your account: lead capture forms and funnels, pipelines, workflow automations, SMS and email follow-up, calendar booking and reminders, AI chatbots and voice agents, and reporting. A good expert also writes the messages, makes sure SMS is A2P compliant, tests every path and documents the build so your team can run it.

Can you set up GoHighLevel from scratch for my business?

Yes. A full setup typically includes business profile and domain settings, phone numbers and A2P registration, email sending domain, users and permissions, custom fields and values, pipelines, calendars, forms and surveys, funnels or website pages, core workflows (speed-to-lead, reminders, nurture, no-show and review requests), and an AI chatbot if needed. Everything is tested and handed over with documentation.

Can you fix or clean up my existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes, this is one of our most requested services. We audit your workflows, triggers, tags, custom fields and pipelines; fix broken or conflicting automations; remove duplicates; tighten your messaging; and leave you with a clear map of what runs where.

Do you build GoHighLevel snapshots for agencies?

Yes. We build and refine snapshots for agencies that want to onboard new sub-accounts quickly. That includes pipelines, workflows, funnels, calendars, custom values and templates, with naming conventions that keep things easy to update across clients.

Can you migrate us to GoHighLevel from HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Keap or another CRM?

Yes. We plan the migration first: what data moves, how fields and tags map, which automations need rebuilding, and how to avoid gaps in follow-up during the switch. Then we import contacts and opportunities, rebuild the key workflows in GoHighLevel and test before going live.

Can you build funnels, websites and landing pages in GoHighLevel?

Yes. We design and build conversion-focused funnels, landing pages and websites directly in GoHighLevel. Every form, survey and calendar is connected to your CRM and automations, so new leads are tagged, followed up and tracked from the first click.

A2P 10DLC & SMS

What is A2P 10DLC and why does GoHighLevel require it?

A2P 10DLC (Application-to-Person, 10-Digit Long Code) is the US carrier registration system for businesses that send text messages from standard local phone numbers. Carriers require it to reduce spam. Without an approved brand and campaign registration, messages to US numbers can be blocked, filtered or heavily limited, which is why GoHighLevel asks you to register.

Can you help get my A2P registration approved?

Yes. Most rejections come from mismatched business details, vague campaign descriptions, weak sample messages, or a website that’s missing a privacy policy, terms or clear opt-in language. We prepare the registration properly, fix your forms and website consent wording, and resubmit with the right use case and samples.

Why are my GoHighLevel SMS messages not delivering?

The most common causes are an unregistered or pending A2P campaign, messages that don’t match the registered use case, missing opt-out language, link shorteners, sending too many messages too fast, or contacts on DND. We review your registration, templates and workflow settings to find and fix the issue.

What is missed-call text-back?

Missed-call text-back automatically sends an SMS to anyone whose call you miss, for example: “Sorry we missed your call! How can we help?” It keeps the conversation going instead of losing the lead to the next business on Google. It can be combined with an AI chatbot that replies, qualifies the lead and books an appointment.

AI chatbots & voice agents

What is Conversation AI in GoHighLevel?

Conversation AI is HighLevel’s AI chatbot. It can reply to messages across SMS, website chat, Facebook, Instagram and other channels, answer questions using your business information, collect details and book appointments. We train it on your services, tone and FAQs, set clear rules for what it should and shouldn’t say, and add a human handoff for anything sensitive.

What is the HighLevel AI Employee?

AI Employee is HighLevel’s suite of AI tools that work inside your account, including Conversation AI, Voice AI, content and workflow AI features, and AI agents. Used well, it handles repetitive conversations and admin so your team can focus on closing and delivering.

Can a Voice AI agent answer calls and book appointments for my business?

Yes. A GoHighLevel Voice AI agent can answer inbound calls, greet callers naturally, answer common questions, capture details, book appointments into your calendar and trigger follow-up workflows. It can also transfer to a human when needed. We script it, test it with real call scenarios and refine it after launch.

Will AI replies sound robotic to my customers?

Not when they’re set up properly. We write the bot’s personality, tone and boundaries based on how your best team member talks, keep replies short and helpful, and test lots of real-world questions before launch. You stay in control and can review conversations anytime.

Email marketing

Which email marketing platforms do you work with?

We build email automation in Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Omnisend, MailerLite, HubSpot and GoHighLevel’s own email builder, and we can connect them to your CRM, store or forms when needed.

What email automations should every business have?

At a minimum: a welcome or onboarding sequence, a lead nurture sequence, appointment or purchase confirmations, a re-engagement (win-back) flow and a review or referral request. E-commerce brands should add abandoned cart, browse abandonment and post-purchase flows. These usually drive the most revenue for the least ongoing effort.

Klaviyo or GoHighLevel for email: which should I use?

Klaviyo is excellent for e-commerce, especially Shopify, thanks to deep product and purchase data. GoHighLevel is ideal for service businesses that want email, SMS, pipelines and booking in one place. Many businesses use both. We’ll recommend what fits your model, not what’s trendy.

Can you improve my email deliverability?

Yes. We check domain authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), sending domain setup, list hygiene, engagement-based segmentation, sending frequency and content, then fix what’s hurting your inbox placement.
