Before you book

Quick answers.

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. It’s a 30-minute conversation about your CRM, your leads and your goals. You leave with clear next steps whether or not we work together.

Which CRMs do you work with?

GoHighLevel is our specialist platform, and we also build in HubSpot, Zoho, Follow Up Boss and other popular CRMs, plus email platforms like Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign and Mailchimp, connected with Zapier, Make or webhooks.

What should I prepare for the call?

Nothing formal. It helps to know where your leads come from, what happens after someone enquires, and where you feel things slip. If you can share screen access to your CRM, even better.

Do you work with clients in my time zone?

Yes. We work with clients around the world, schedule calls at times that suit you and share async updates so work keeps moving between calls.
