My job is to close those gaps, quietly, in the background, so you stop losing customers you already paid to attract.

Why I do this

Good businesses lose customers to slow follow-up. I fix that.

I started out building workflows for businesses that were already spending money on ads, referrals and content, yet still watching leads go cold. The offer was rarely the issue. The gap was always the same: what happens in the minutes, days and weeks after someone says “I’m interested”.

That’s why I focus on the job, not the software. Whether you run GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho or something else, the work is the same: capture every lead, reply fast, follow up like a human, book the call and keep the data clean. GoHighLevel is where I’m most at home, but I’ll always start with what you have.

I understand the business first and build second. Before anything goes live, every path is tested the way a real customer would use it. Then I hand it over with documentation and walkthrough videos, and stay on if you want ongoing support.
